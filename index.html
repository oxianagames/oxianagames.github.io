The game

One town. One hidden family. Until only one side is left.

You walk the streets in first person and talk to whoever is close enough to hear. Someone at the table is lying. The city will tell you who, if you know where to look.

Talk

Proximity voice. Whisper in a doorway, shout across the square. Who you stand next to is a choice.

Watch

Who went into the pharmacy at midnight? Who came out with wet shoes? Memory is your only camera.

Read the city

Footprints on wet stone, a torn ledger page, a newspaper at dawn. The street keeps receipts.

Roles

Same faces every night. Never the same hand.

Fourteen fixed characters live in Port Sable. Roles are shuffled every match, so the barber you trusted yesterday may be the one holding the knife tonight.

OXIANA GAMES is a small studio in Tashkent, Uzbekistan, building Dawn Will Tell in Unreal Engine 5 with a production line of AI coding agents and human direction: people decide the design, automation builds, tests and reviews every change before it lands.

Questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every role, map and rule is free. Paid items are cosmetics only and never change the odds.

What do I need to play?

A Windows PC, a microphone and friends. Steam first; mobile comes after launch with shared accounts.

How many players?

4 to 12 in one match, about 25 minutes. Bots fill in only when somebody leaves.

When does it come out?

The target is early 2027 on Steam, free to play. There is no official date yet; playtesters hear first.

Is there an age rating?

Not yet rated. The game is written for players 16 and up: a 1950s crime city, no gore, no real-world brands or people.

Can I stream it?

Please do, from the first playtest on. Every match ends with the Truth replay, made for exactly that.

What happens to my email?

We read it, reply, and delete it when the playtest is over. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.
