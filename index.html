Dawn Will Tell

A free-to-play multiplayer social deduction game in a living 1950s harbor city. On PC first; the Steam page opens in early 2027.

Day. Night. Dawn. Trial.

By day you talk, walk and lie. By night the killers move. At dawn the town puts someone on trial.

One round runs through all four hours. Pick an hour to see the city then.

Why it plays different

  1. Every face is a player.

    There are no NPCs. The barber, the docker, the woman under the lamp: each one is a real person, and some of them are lying.

  2. The dead walk on.

    Killed players stay in the city as ghosts. They see the rest of the night play out and wait for the truth at dawn.

  3. The secrets stay on the server.

    A dedicated server holds every role and decides every outcome. Each player's machine gets only what that player may know, so no one can read another player's role from their own game.
