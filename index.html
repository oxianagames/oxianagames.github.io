The Port Sable Ledger

Dawn edition · printed while you slept

Body found on Harbour Row; nobody saw anything, everybody knows something

Three sets of prints survived the rain. One of them walked back toward the pharmacy.

The dock crews found him at first light under the Harbour Row sign, hat still on. One shot was heard from Cannery Street, a little before the lamps came back on.

The hotel register shows four guests after dusk. The switchboard log shows one call, never answered. The pharmacy book shows a sale of iodine to a customer who did not sign.

The Ledger does not accuse. The Ledger prints what the city knows. The trial opens at nine. Ballots will be cast in the open, as is the custom of this town.

Every dawn the game prints a paper from the real events of your match. The names in yours will be your friends'.

Questions · from the town hall

Before you accuse anybody.

Is it really free?

Yes. Cosmetics are bought with Sable, the in-game coin. Nothing for sale changes roles, odds or information.

How many players?

Four to twelve. Roles scale with the group.

Do I need a microphone?

It helps a lot. Voice fades with distance, so whispering in a doorway is a real tactic. Text works too.

Is there a campaign?

No. Every match writes its own story, and the Truth tells it back to you.

Which platforms?

PC on Steam first. iOS and Android later, same account, same wardrobe.

How violent is it?

16+. A shot in the dark, a body under a coat. No blood.

When can I play?

Private playtests now, by invitation. Steam page next.
